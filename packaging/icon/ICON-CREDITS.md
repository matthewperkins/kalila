# kalila icon credits

The kalila icon (`kalila-icon.svg` and the `kalila-*.png` renders) is adapted from two drawings on
Wikimedia Commons:

- **Bastet** by Gunawan Kartapranata (Gunkarta),
  [File:Bastet.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bastet.svg), licensed
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- **Sekhmet** by Jeff Dahl,
  [File:Sekhmet.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sekhmet.svg), licensed
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (one of several licences
  the author offers).

The unchanged originals are in `src/`.

**Changes:** both heads cropped; Bastet mirrored and set lower; both placed on a rounded tile
with a gold border and a fade at the bottom; two traces added (a slow wave leaving Bastet's
pupil, in her iris blue, and an action potential entering Sekhmet's pupil, in her iris
red-orange); each iris and pupil redrawn over its trace. `make_icon.py` builds the icon from
the originals.

**Licence of the icon:** [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
This applies to the icon files only, not to the kalila source code.
